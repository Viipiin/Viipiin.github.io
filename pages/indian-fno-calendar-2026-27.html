F&O ban & MWPL

MWPL is now the lower of 15% of free-float shares or 65× average daily delivery value (floor of 10% of free float), checked several times a day, not only at close.

Ban trigger: a stock enters the ban when delta-based (future-equivalent) open interest crosses 95% of MWPL. It exits when FutEq OI falls below 80%.

During a ban you can only reduce positions (square-off, roll). Anything that raises your net FutEq exposure is blocked and can be penalised. FutEq OI can rise with price moves alone, because option deltas change.

Physical settlement (stock F&O)

All stock futures and in-the-money stock options settle by delivery. Futures and ITM long calls/short puts mean you take delivery; the reverse means you deliver shares.

Exchanges raise delivery margins over the last 4 trading days before expiry, and many brokers square off or block ITM positions in that window. The calendar marks it with a teal underline. Check your broker's cut-off.
